# semantic shape

a machine learning research submodule of the [biosemiotic-refractor](https://github.com/ronniross/biosemiotic-refractor) repository.


Language shapes thought.  It channels attention. It makes some ways of seeing the world easier and others nearly impossible to articulate.

If extremism is a kind of epistemic pollution—a flattening of complexity, a reduction of others into categories, a closure of thought—then what patterns of language might restore our ability to think with complexity, empathy, and humility?
